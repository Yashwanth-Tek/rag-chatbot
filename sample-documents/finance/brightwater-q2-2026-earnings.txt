HEADLINE RESULTS (quarter ended 30 June 2026)
Total revenue was $412.6 million, up 8.4 percent from $380.6 million in the second quarter of 2025.
Net income was $68.3 million, up 11.2 percent year over year.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.42, compared with $1.27 a year earlier.
Return on equity was 13.8 percent.

REVENUE BY SEGMENT
Retail Banking: $198.1 million (up 6.1 percent). Deposits grew to $21.4 billion.
Wealth Management: $121.4 million (up 14.9 percent). Assets under management reached $58.7 billion.
Commercial Lending: $93.1 million (up 5.2 percent). The loan book stood at $9.8 billion.

KEY RATIOS
Net interest margin: 3.21 percent (3.08 percent in Q2 2025).
Efficiency ratio: 57.4 percent (59.9 percent in Q2 2025).
Common equity tier 1 (CET1) capital ratio: 12.6 percent.
Non-performing loans: 0.84 percent of total loans.

CAPITAL RETURNS
The board declared a quarterly dividend of $0.38 per share, payable on 15 September 2026, and approved a share buyback of up to $150 million over the next 12 months.

OUTLOOK
Brightwater raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to between $1.62 billion and $1.66 billion, from $1.58 billion to $1.63 billion previously. Expenses are expected to grow by 3 to 4 percent for the year.

CHIEF EXECUTIVE'S COMMENT
"Wealth Management delivered its strongest quarter on record, and disciplined cost control lifted our efficiency ratio below 58 percent for the first time," said Chief Executive Officer Dana Whitfield.

RISKS
Results could be affected by changes in interest rates, a slowdown in commercial real estate, higher credit losses, cybersecurity incidents and new regulation. These forward-looking statements are not guarantees of future performance.
