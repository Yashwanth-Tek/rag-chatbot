# Riverside Telehealth: frequently asked questions

_Fictional sample document created for testing the RAG chatbot. All organisations, people, figures and contact details in it are invented._

## What is Riverside Telehealth?

Riverside Telehealth lets you see a Riverside General Hospital clinician by video, using the CareLink app or a web browser. Visits usually last 15 to 20 minutes.

## When is it available?

Monday to Friday from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. The service is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Which problems can be handled by telehealth?

Telehealth is suitable for cold and flu symptoms, minor rashes, repeat prescriptions for stable long-term conditions, mental health check-ins, and follow-up visits after a hospital stay. Under the hospital's discharge policy, low-risk patients may have their follow-up by telehealth.

It is not suitable for chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, sudden weakness or confusion, or any other emergency. In an emergency, call 911 straight away.

## What do I need for a video visit?

A smartphone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone, an internet connection of at least 5 Mbps, and a quiet, private place. Open the CareLink app 15 minutes before your visit to test your connection.

## How much does it cost?

Most insurance plans cover telehealth visits, and your copay is the same as for an in-person visit. Patients without insurance pay a flat fee of $49 per visit.

## Can I get a prescription?

Yes, for many medicines. Prescriptions are sent electronically to the pharmacy you choose. Under Riverside policy, controlled substances are never prescribed through telehealth.

## How do I cancel or reschedule?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your visit, in the CareLink app or by calling (555) 010-4420. Late cancellations and missed visits are charged a $25 fee.

## Is my visit private?

Yes. Video visits are encrypted and the platform is HIPAA compliant. Visits are never recorded.

## Can a family member join my visit?

Yes. You can invite up to two additional people with a link from the CareLink app.

## What if the clinician decides I need to be seen in person?

They will book you an in-person appointment within 48 hours, or direct you to the nearest urgent care center if you need to be seen sooner.
